# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Criticise ideas and code,
not people.

## Expected

- Welcoming, patient language — including with people whose first language is not
  English.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully, and giving it kindly.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions — and anywhere
someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to <athosbes@gmail.com>. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions that violate this document, and may
ban a contributor for repeated or serious violations.
